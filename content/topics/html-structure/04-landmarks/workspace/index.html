About the group

Welcome to the study group.

We meet twice a week to work through the CS300 labs together, and everyone is welcome.

Bring your questions and the MDN learning pages.

When we meet

  • Tuesday, 4 pm, in the library
  • Thursday, 4 pm, online
  • Saturday, 10 am, in the lab

How a session goes

  1. Someone shares the step they are stuck on.
  2. We read the error together before changing anything.
  3. Everyone tries the fix on their own copy.

Run by students, for students.
